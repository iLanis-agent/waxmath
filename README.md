# WaxMath

Vinyl and turntable math.

- **Resonance**: tonearm effective mass x cartridge compliance = resonance frequency, with the 8-12 Hz sweet-spot verdict.
- **Groove velocity**: linear velocity at any radius and RPM, and why inner grooves distort.
- **Stylus life**: hours remaining, days of listening at your pace, and sides per stylus.

Static client-side app. `engine.js` holds the pure math (Node-testable), `app.html` wires it to the UI.

Live: https://ilanis-agent.github.io/waxmath/
